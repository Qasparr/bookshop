## IV. KNOWN DEFECTS OF THE OCR LAYER (declared, not hidden)

The djvu text is machine OCR of a 1668 folio, confidence 74 per the
Archive's own page index. Defect classes, all handled only as the
build plan declares:

1. **Long-s conflation.** The 1668 press prints ſ for s (round s
   finally); the OCR renders ſ as "f" ("fenfu" for *senſu*, "eft" for
   *eft* = est with ſ). Restoration is a normalization, performed
   only from the builder's printed substitution table + protected
   genuine-f stems; unresolved tokens stand as the OCR gave them
   and are counted in each brick's apparatus as residue. The Latin
   is otherwise reproduced in its own orthography (u/v, i/j,
   ligatures as the OCR carries them) — it is NOT respelled into
   this house's epigraphic Latin; that rule governs the house's own
   Latin only.
2. **Drop-cap mangling.** Decorative initials are split or lost in
   the OCR (the Introduction's [N]aturam stands as "Aturam" with
   border debris). Restorations are bracketed and listed per brick.
3. **Running heads and page furniture interleaved** in the text
   flow, including one head at printed p. 13 reading "Cap. 4" amid
   Capvt III's closing passage — flagged as a collation locus in
   Brick 1, not silently corrected (adjudication against the
   facsimile leaf is UNCHARTED).
4. **Margin-note interleave.** The 1668 marginal summaries are
   sometimes fused into body lines by the OCR (e.g. a mangled
   "Conjectura eventi" fragment in Capvt III). Fused fragments are
   removed only where the corresponding English margin note in
   #3207 fixes the locus, and every removal is listed.
5. **Display-type noise** in headings and the contents leaves
   (spaced capitals, stray glyphs). The edition sets its own
   headings from the census in §III; the OCR's heading debris is
   never set as text.
6. **Hyphenation and spacing artifacts** of the folio lineation —
   resolved by the declared typographic normalizations (line-end
   dehyphenation, punctuation spacing), which change no words.
7. **The ampersand machinery:** "&" prints for *et*; intra-word "&"
   is the OCR's reading of the ct-ligature ("Do&rinam" = Doctrinam);
   "&c" and its OCR doubles ("8c", "6c") print for *etc.* Declared
   as a class; applied mechanically.

  (the same scan's text PDF) permits spot adjudication of named
  loci; a leaf-by-leaf collation of the OCR against the facsimile
  has NOT been performed and is not claimed anywhere in this

  a single-witness OCR text and is labeled so.
- **No Latin was fabricated, completed, or retranslated** at any
  locus. Where the OCR fails beyond the declared repairs, the
  edition prints the failure as apparatus, not as Hobbes.
