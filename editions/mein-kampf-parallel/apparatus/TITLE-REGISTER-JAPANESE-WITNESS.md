# TITLE REGISTER — ENTRY J-1: THE JAPANESE FORMS OF THE TITLE

DOCTRINE: a critical edition registers the names its text has
borne in other tongues, because the title is the first translation
and the first interpretation. The German *Mein Kampf* entered
Japanese three times in the period; the three title forms are
historical facts of the book's reception and are registered here
as such — cited bibliographically, never reproduced as text.

## THE WITNESS OF RECORD

**『吾が鬪爭』** — the old-character (kyūjitai) form of the title.
Translation by **Manabe Ryōichi (眞鍋良一)**. Tokyo: **Kōfūkan,
1942**. Selected by the founder as this edition's Japanese
witness of record: the wartime form, in the characters as printed
before the post-war script reforms (鬪 for modern 闘, 爭 for 争).

## THE REGISTER — THREE HISTORICAL JAPANESE FORMS

1. **『余の闘争』** — *Yo no Tōsō*. The FIRST Japanese edition:
   translation by Sakai Ryūji, Tokyo: Naigai-sha, 1932. The title
   renders the possessive in the archaic first person 余 (*yo*).
2. **『我が闘争』** — *Waga Tōsō*. The Tōa Kenkyūjo (East Asia
   Research Institute) translation, 1942; reprinted 2018. The
   title in modern characters, 我が for the possessive.
3. **『吾が鬪爭』** — *Waga Tōsō*. The Manabe Ryōichi translation,
   Tokyo: Kōfūkan, 1942 — entry above, the witness of record.
   Same reading as form 2; the difference is the characters:
   吾 for 我, and the old forms 鬪爭 for 闘争. The register prints
   the distinction because in this material the distinction is
   the datum: two 1942 translations, two title forms, one year.

any jurisdiction. Consequences, binding on this edition:

- The Manabe translation is **CITED BIBLIOGRAPHICALLY ONLY** —
  title, translator, publisher, year — as in this entry.
- **No part of its text is reproduced** as body text, parallel
  text, quotation, or specimen anywhere in this edition.
- The witness-of-record designation is a REGISTRATIVE act (the

The same discipline is noted for the other two forms: the 1932

likewise cited by title, translator or body, publisher, and year
only; no text of either is reproduced in this edition.
